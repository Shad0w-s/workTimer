# Flip Timer

A full-screen flip-clock study timer inspired by Flocus. Open `index.html` in any browser. Nothing to install.

## Features
- **Flip clock**: animated split-flap minutes and seconds (hours appear automatically for long timers).
- **Quick timers**: one-tap presets (5–60 min) or a custom h/m/s time, with an editable label.
- **Programs (chains)**: build sequences like *Study 10 → Break 5*, repeat them N times, and save them by name.
  - Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder steps (mouse or touch), or use ↑ / duplicate / delete.
  - Unnamed steps show as "Timer 1", "Timer 2", … and can be renamed to anything.
  - Each step can have its own end sound, so study and break endings sound different.
  - Click any step chip under the clock to jump to it.
- **Sounds**: 7 built-in alarm sounds with volume and preview, optional "keep ringing until dismissed", optional tick, and desktop notifications.
- **Themes**: 9 color themes, 22 scenic photo backgrounds, upload your own image, and a dimming slider.

## Shortcuts
`Space` start/pause · `R` reset · `S` skip step · `F` fullscreen · `T` timer panel · `P` programs · `1–8` presets · `Esc` close

Settings, saved programs and your custom background are stored in your browser (localStorage).
